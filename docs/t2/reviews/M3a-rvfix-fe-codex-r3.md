- **P1 — A rename followed while the form is clean can still bypass a later unsaved-changes guard.** [unsavedChanges.tsx:79](/Users/richie/code/quickr-worktrees/t2-m3a-rvfix-fe/frontend/src/lib/unsavedChanges.tsx:79) only consumes the one-shot token when `shouldBlockNavigation` is true. If the rename is followed while the form is clean—or on a page without a guard—the token remains in `pageKeepers` on the destination history entry. After navigating away, editing another form and going back to that entry, the guard consumes the still-valid token and permits navigation, losing the new edits.
