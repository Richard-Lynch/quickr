# M1 evidence · timers v2

This file maps every acceptance criterion in
[`docs/t2/M1-plan.md`](../M1-plan.md) (revision 4) to its proof. The base is
`t2/m1` at `da126790`: every M1 WP and both milestone-review fix PRs are merged
there.

**Sources**

- **Merge SHAs** are copied from
  `git log --merges origin/feat/t2..origin/t2/m1`. Three PRs have no merge
  commit of their own on `t2/m1`, and their rows say so:
  - #180 was squash-merged;
  - #157, #160 and #184 merged into stack branches.
- **Test names and CI links** are taken from each PR's body. Where a body had no
  link, it comes from the PR's comments or its check rollup, and the row says
  so.

Each WP's evidence lives in its PR: the commands, counts, Codex file and
red-first commit. This file indexes that evidence. It does not repeat it.

**Gaps.** Every criterion without full proof is marked **GAP** in its row. They
are all listed together in
[Criteria without full proof](#criteria-without-full-proof).
